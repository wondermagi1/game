# V10 scene art

The six chapter backgrounds in `scenes/` are AI-assisted original assets generated specifically for Threefold Trial on 2026-10-04. They were prompted as original top-down environments and do not reuse extracted assets from reference games. The room renderer adds a low-opacity readability veil, gameplay borders, cover geometry, portals and chapter-specific ambient particles at runtime.

Themes: jade monastery, emberwood foundry, runic archive, crimson shrine, meridian engine hall and starfall observatory. Source generation outputs remain outside the repository; the imported PNG files are the game-ready masters.
