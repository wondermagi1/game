# 日式 2.5D 像素庭院生成记录

工具：内置 imagegen。原始生成图片保留在本机 `.codex/generated_images`；本项目保存实际使用的最终图与两张编辑母稿。

## 地表布局初稿

Production game ground map for a Godot 2D top-down Japanese garden action roguelike, not a concept mockup. Landscape 4:3 canvas, orthographic elevated view, no horizon. Entire canvas is one contiguous explorable landscape. Ground layer only: no houses, gates, tall trees, characters, enemies, UI or text. Broad stone courtyard at x32–66%, y37–67%, connected horizontal and vertical stone paths through the centre. Extra connected stepping-stone loops in lower-left and upper-right grass. Enclosed turquoise koi pond in upper right, a small pond in lower left, neither blocks the cross paths. Moss, low ferns, tiny flowers and restrained sakura petals. Jade/olive/celadon greens, warm ash stone, soft teal water, tiny coral-pink accents, light from upper left, quiet readable central paving.

Initial world-space layout was authored at 4096×3072, then tuned to a 3200×2400 playable sample so props, pixel density and traversal distances match the existing characters and viewport.

## 景物初稿与排布修整

Production sprite atlas, transparent landscape canvas 1536×1024. Six separate Japanese 2.5D props, three columns and two rows: top timber shrine, torii, cherry tree; bottom bamboo grove, stone lantern, small shrine monument. Orthographic elevated front view shows top surfaces and substantial front/side faces. Stable bottom-centre foot contact. One light from upper left, teal ceramic roofs, warm timber, amber shoji windows, mossy ivory stone, jade leaves and pink canopy. No characters, text, labels or ground patches. Transparent holes in torii and foliage. Repack with generous transparent gutters; complete branches and roofs fit each region without clipping. Keep the original designs, silhouettes and camera.
